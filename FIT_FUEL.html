How Does The BMI Scale Work?

BMI (Body Mass Index) compares your weight to your height. Here is the standard medical breakdown so you understand exactly what your numbers mean:

Under 18.5
Underweight
Weight is too low for your height. Need to eat more calories.
18.5 - 24.9
Normal Weight
Healthy ratio of height and weight. Best for overall longevity.
25.0 - 29.9
Overweight
Slightly heavier than recommended. Slight diet tweaks needed.
30.0 +
Obese
High body fat risk. Strict caloric deficit highly recommended.
