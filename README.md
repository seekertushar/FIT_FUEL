FIT FUEL is a web-based fitness and diet management application designed to help
gym-going individuals plan, track, and achieve their fitness goals, whether that goal
is weight loss, weight gain, or weight maintenance. Many people who join a gym
struggle to find a diet plan suited to their body type, current weight, and target
weight, and often rely on generic, non-personalized information scattered across the
internet. FIT FUEL addresses this gap by offering a single platform where a user can
register, enter basic health details such as height, current weight, and target weight,
and instantly receive a calculated Body Mass Index (BMI) along with a suggested
diet chart and workout routine aligned with their goal.
The project is built purely using front-end and back-end web development
technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and a lightweight server-side stack), with no
artificial intelligence or machine learning components involved, as required by the
course scope. The system stores user profiles, diet templates, workout templates, and
progress logs, allowing users to track their transformation over time through a simple
dashboard. The significance of the project lies in making structured fitness guidance
accessible, affordable, and easy to use for students, working professionals, and gym
enthusiasts who cannot always afford a personal trainer or dietician.
